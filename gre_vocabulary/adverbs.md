### Sharply

- **Part of speech:** adv
- **Definition:** in a clear, sudden, intense, or stylishly neat way; sharply appointed means smartly or elegantly dressed or arranged
- **Synonyms:** keenly, distinctly, smartly
- **Example:** The speaker was sharply appointed in a dark suit and polished shoes.
