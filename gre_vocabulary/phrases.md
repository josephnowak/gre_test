### Astonished by

- **Part of speech:** phrase
- **Definition:** very surprised because of something
- **Synonyms:** amazed by, stunned by, shocked by
- **Example:** The researchers were astonished by the speed of the recovery.
- **Source:** Supplemental word list

### Dubious of

- **Part of speech:** phrase
- **Definition:** doubtful or skeptical about something
- **Synonyms:** skeptical of, doubtful about, suspicious of
- **Example:** The board was dubious of the proposal because the data were incomplete.
- **Source:** Supplemental word list

### Entitled to

- **Part of speech:** phrase
- **Definition:** having a right or legitimate claim to something
- **Synonyms:** eligible for, authorized to, allowed to claim
- **Example:** Employees are entitled to a written explanation of the policy.
- **Source:** Supplemental word list

### Off and on

- **Part of speech:** adv phrase
- **Definition:** occasionally or intermittently, with pauses between occurrences
- **Synonyms:** intermittently, occasionally, now and then
- **Example:** She studied Latin off and on for several years.
- **Source:** Supplemental word list
