### Aberrant

- **Part of speech:** adj
- **Definition:** abnormal or deviant; departing from an accepted standard
- **Synonyms:** deviant, divergent, abnormal, atypical
- **Example:** Due to the aberrant outcome of experiment 12, we concluded there was likely an error in how it was conducted and decided not to include its results with the previous 11 experiments.
- **Source:** Subeezy Vocabulary Set 5.pdf

### Admonitory

- **Part of speech:** adj
- **Definition:** serving to warn; giving or conveying a warning or reprimand, especially as corrective
- **Synonyms:** cautionary, cautioning, exemplary, monitory, premonitory, warning
- **Example:** Prior to leaving for a party, the parents gave their daughter an admonitory lecture, warning of the myriad dangers and encouraging her to behave safely.
