# Rounding

## Basic rule

To round a number to a given place value, look at the digit immediately to the right of that place:

- If that digit is 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4, keep the target digit unchanged.
- If that digit is 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9, increase the target digit by 1.

After that, replace all digits to the right of the target place with 0 if you are rounding a whole number, or remove them if you are writing a decimal form.

## Examples

**Round 0.446 to 1 decimal place:**
- The target digit is the tenths digit: 4
- The next digit is 4
- Since 4 < 5, we keep the tenths digit
- **Result: 0.4**

**Round 0.456 to 1 decimal place:**
- The target digit is again the tenths digit: 4
- The next digit is 5
- Since 5 >= 5, we increase the tenths digit by 1
- **Result: 0.5**

## Worked word problem

**Problem:**
Jessica sells lemons on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Nicole sells apples on Tuesdays and Thursdays. If Jessica sells 8 lemons per day and Nicole sells 4 apples per day, what is the tens digit of the total number of fruit sold by both Jessica and Nicole in exactly 4 weeks?
Assume each week runs from Sunday through Saturday.

**Solution:**
Jessica works 3 days per week, so in 4 weeks she works:
3 * 4 = 12 days
She sells:
12 * 8 = 96 lemons

Nicole works 2 days per week, so in 4 weeks she works:
2 * 4 = 8 days
She sells:
8 * 4 = 32 apples

Total fruit sold:
96 + 32 = 128

The tens digit of 128 is **2**.
