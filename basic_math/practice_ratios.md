# Ratio Practice Exercises

This file contains practice exercises for four common types of ratio and rate problems.

---

## Type 1: Multiple Ratios Linkage (The Chain Method)

*Goal: Combine multiple related ratios (A:B, B:C, etc.) into a single chain (A:B:C) to find unknown quantities.*

### Exercise 1.1: Paint Mixing
In a paint mixture, the ratio of Red to Yellow is **3 : 2**, and the ratio of Yellow to Blue is **5 : 4**. If you have **15 liters** of Red paint, how many liters of Blue paint do you need?

### Exercise 1.2: Fruit Basket
A fruit basket has apples to bananas in ratio **2 : 3**. Bananas to oranges ratio is **4 : 5**. If there are **30 oranges**, how many apples are there?

### Exercise 1.3: Bakery Recipe
In a bakery, the ratio of flour to sugar is **5 : 2**, sugar to butter is **3 : 1**, and butter to vanilla is **4 : 1**. If a large batch uses **2 cups** of vanilla, how much flour is used?

### Exercise 1.4: Staffing
A company has the ratio of Managers to Engineers as **1 : 6**, and Engineers to Technicians as **3 : 4**. If there are **48 Technicians**, what is the total number of these employees (Managers + Engineers + Technicians)?

### Exercise 1.5: Farm Animals
On a farm, the ratio of cows to sheep is **4 : 5**, sheep to goats is **3 : 2**, and goats to chickens is **5 : 8**. If there are **40 chickens**, how many cows are there?

## Type 2: Part-Whole and Sub-group Ratios

*Goal: Use the total quantity to find parts, and then apply secondary ratios to those parts.*

### Exercise 2.1: School Sports
In a school of **240 students**, the ratio of boys to girls is **5 : 7**. If the ratio of boys who play sports to those who don't is **3 : 2**, how many boys play sports?

### Exercise 2.2: Library Mystery
A library has **500 books**. The ratio of fiction to non-fiction is **3 : 2**. Among fiction books, the ratio of mystery to romance is **4 : 1**. How many mystery books are in the library?

### Exercise 2.3: Committee Demographics
A committee of **64 people** has a ratio of men to women of **5 : 3**. If **1/4** of the women are over 60 years old, how many women are 60 or younger?

### Exercise 2.4: Animal Shelter
The ratio of cats to dogs in a shelter is **2 : 3**, and there are **80 animals** in total (only cats and dogs). If the ratio of male cats to female cats is **1 : 3**, how many female cats are there?

### Exercise 2.5: Parking Lot
In a parking lot with **150 vehicles**, the ratio of cars to trucks is **7 : 3**. If the ratio of electric cars to gas cars is **2 : 3**, how many electric cars are in the lot?

## Type 3: Rates and Unit Conversions

*Goal: Combine rates (e.g., speed, fuel efficiency) with time or distance to solve for a specific quantity.*

### Exercise 3.1: Road Trip
A car travels **35 miles per gallon**. It travels at a constant speed of **70 miles per hour**. If the trip takes **4 hours and 30 minutes**, how many gallons of gas were used?

### Exercise 3.2: Printer Ink
A printer can print **20 pages per minute**. The ratio of ink used to pages printed is **1 : 50** (1 ml per 50 pages). If the printer runs for **2.5 hours**, how much ink is consumed?

### Exercise 3.3: Running Calories
A runner covers **8 miles per hour**. For every mile run, they burn **120 calories**. If they run for **1 hour and 15 minutes**, how many calories do they burn?

### Exercise 3.4: Water Leak
A pipe leaks water at a rate of **2 gallons per hour**. There are **128 ounces** in a gallon. How many ounces of water leak in **45 minutes**?

### Exercise 3.5: Spaceship Fuel
A spaceship travels **10,000 km per hour**. It consumes **5 tons** of fuel per **1,000 km**. How much fuel is consumed in a **15-minute** flight?

---

## Type 4: Relative Speed (Closer or Farther Apart)

*Goal: Determine how fast the distance between two moving objects changes when they move toward each other, away from each other, or in the same direction.*

### Exercise 4.1: Trains Approaching
Two trains start **210 miles** apart and travel toward each other on the same track. One train moves at **55 mph** and the other at **50 mph**. How long will it take for them to meet?

### Exercise 4.2: Hikers Separating
Two hikers leave the same cabin at the same time and walk in opposite directions. One walks at **3 mph** and the other at **4.5 mph**. How far apart are they after **2 hours and 40 minutes**?

### Exercise 4.3: Rescue Boat
A rescue boat is **18 miles** behind a fishing boat, and both travel in the same direction. The rescue boat moves at **24 mph** and the fishing boat moves at **18 mph**. How long does it take for the rescue boat to catch the fishing boat?

### Exercise 4.4: Car Chasing a Bus
A bus leaves a station and travels at **50 mph**. **30 minutes later**, a car leaves the same station on the same road at **65 mph**. How long after the car starts will it catch the bus?

### Exercise 4.5: Drones Flying Apart
Two drones start at the same tower and fly in opposite directions. One flies at **12 m/s** and the other at **18 m/s**. How far apart are they after **90 seconds**?

## Solutions and Step-by-Step Guide

### Type 1 Solutions
*   **1.1:** R:Y = 3:2, Y:B = 5:4. Chain: R:Y:B = 15:10:8. If R=15, then 15 (actual) ÷ 15 (ratio) = 1. Blue = 8 * 1 = **8 liters**.
*   **1.2:** A:B = 2:3, B:O = 4:5. Chain: A:B:O = 8:12:15. If O=30, then 30 (actual) ÷ 15 (ratio) = 2. Apples = 8 * 2 = **16 apples**.
*   **1.3:** Chain F:S:Bt:V = 30:12:4:1. If V=2, then 2 (actual) ÷ 1 (ratio) = 2. Flour = 30 * 2 = **60 cups**.
*   **1.4:** Chain M:E:T = 1:6:8. T=48, then 48 (actual) ÷ 8 (ratio) = 6. Total parts = 15. Total = 15 * 6 = **90 employees**.
*   **1.5:** Chain C:S:G:Ch = 12:15:10:16. Ch=40, then 40 (actual) ÷ 16 (ratio) = 2.5. Cows = 12 * 2.5 = **30 cows**.

### Type 2 Solutions
*   **2.1:** Total = 240. B:G = 5:7. Total parts = 12. Boys = 240 * (5/12) = 100. Sports:No-Sports = 3:2. Sports = 100 * (3/5) = **60 boys**.
*   **2.2:** Total = 500. F:NF = 3:2. Total parts = 5. Fiction = 500 * (3/5) = 300. Mystery:Romance = 4:1. Mystery = 300 * (4/5) = **240 books**.
*   **2.3:** Total = 64. M:W = 5:3. Total parts = 8. Women = 64 * (3/8) = 24. Women 60 or younger = 24 * (3/4) = **18 women**.
*   **2.4:** Total = 80. C:D = 2:3. Total parts = 5. Cats = 80 * (2/5) = 32. M:F = 1:3. Female Cats = 32 * (3/4) = **24 female cats**.
*   **2.5:** Total = 150. C:T = 7:3. Total parts = 10. Cars = 150 * (7/10) = 105. E:G = 2:3. Electric = 105 * (2/5) = **42 electric cars**.

### Type 3 Solutions
*   **3.1:** 4.5 hours * 70 mph = 315 miles. 315 miles / 35 mpg = **9 gallons**.
*   **3.2:** 2.5 hours = 150 minutes. 150 min * 20 ppm = 3,000 pages. 3,000 pages / 50 (ratio 1:50) = **60 ml of ink**.
*   **3.3:** 1.25 hours * 8 mph = 10 miles. 10 miles * 120 cal/mile = **1,200 calories**.
*   **3.4:** 45 minutes = 0.75 hours. 0.75 hours * 2 gallons/hour = 1.5 gallons. 1.5 gallons * 128 oz/gallon = **192 ounces**.
*   **3.5:** 15 minutes = 0.25 hours. 0.25 hours * 10,000 km/h = 2,500 km. 2,500 km / 1,000 km * 5 tons = **12.5 tons of fuel**.

### Type 4 Solutions
*   **4.1:** Relative speed = 55 + 50 = 105 mph. Time = 210 / 105 = **2 hours**.
*   **4.2:** 2 hours 40 minutes = 8/3 hours. Relative speed = 3 + 4.5 = 7.5 mph. Distance apart = 7.5 * (8/3) = **20 miles**.
*   **4.3:** Same direction, so use the difference: 24 - 18 = 6 mph. Time = 18 / 6 = **3 hours**.
*   **4.4:** In 30 minutes, the bus gets a head start of 50 * 0.5 = 25 miles. Relative speed = 65 - 50 = 15 mph. Time after the car starts = 25 / 15 = **1 hour 40 minutes**.
*   **4.5:** Relative speed = 12 + 18 = 30 m/s. Distance apart after 90 seconds = 30 * 90 = **2,700 meters**.
